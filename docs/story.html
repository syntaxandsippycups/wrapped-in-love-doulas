The people and purpose behind the care

A village of care,
wrapped in love.

We are a Black woman-led doula and lactation team, building space for families to feel seen, supported, and powerful in their choices.

Our why

Every family deserves to be held with dignity.

Wrapped in Love grew from a simple truth: people deserve care that listens, honors their culture, and trusts them to know their own lives.

We bring emotional, physical, and informational support to moments that can feel tender, joyful, uncertain, or all three at once. Our role is to make room for your voice—and to remind you that you do not have to do this alone.

Founded in relationship.
Rooted in community.

Wrapped in Love is led by co-founders Angelene Love-Adams and Dera Williams.

Angelene Love-Adams

Co-founder · Doula & lactation support

Angelene brings a deep commitment to birth equity, family wellbeing, and support that honors the wisdom already present in every client.

Dera Williams

Co-founder · Doula & community care

Dera shares the work of creating a welcoming village—one that makes practical, culturally grounded care easier to reach.
