01 · PRENATAL + BIRTH

Birth doula support

A familiar, steady presence through preparation, labor, and the first moments after birth.

  • Get to know you, your values, and your hopes
  • Birth preferences and partner preparation
  • Continuous emotional and physical support
  • Advocacy that keeps your voice at the center

02 · AFTER BABY

Postpartum doula care

Hands-on help and gentle check-ins as everyone adjusts to life with a new baby.

  • Recovery, rest, and emotional wellbeing
  • Newborn care and family transitions
  • Meal and household support
  • Referrals and connection to local resources

03 · FEEDING + LACTATION

Feeding support

Care that meets your goals without judgment—whether you’re nursing, pumping, combo feeding, or figuring it out.

  • Latch and positioning support
  • Feeding questions and confidence building
  • Support for changing plans and changing needs

Whole-journey care
